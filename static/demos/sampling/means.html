About these numbers

Daily social-media use is simulated, not collected from real students. This is the same fixed population as the main Sampling Lab: 10,000 hypothetical students with usage times from 0 to 360 minutes per day, recorded to the nearest tenth of a minute. The population average is approximately 108 minutes per day. Students are selected randomly without replacement within each sample, and can be selected again in a later sample.

The top plot shows individual observations in the latest sample. The bottom plot keeps one average per sample. Both use the same horizontal scale. Points are stacked in narrow bins for legibility; the averages are computed from the actual values, not the plotted positions. The individual observations stay visible when their average moves to the bottom plot.

The repeated-sampling animation shows the same three steps at a faster pace. Motion is omitted when your device requests reduced motion. Up to 200 sample means can be plotted.
